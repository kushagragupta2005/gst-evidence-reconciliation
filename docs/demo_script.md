# Demo narration (replacement live capture planned)

Solo participant: Kushagra Gupta. Generated results are authoritative in results.json.

## Problem (opening screen, exact wording)

A GST officer can’t tell which factories are hiding production or inflating tax credits, because every document she checks is declared by the factory itself, while the independent evidence (power use, goods movement, supplier records, bank flows) sits in separate places and is never compared. If this worked, she would start each week with a short list of units whose paperwork contradicts physical reality, each contradiction spelled out, and honest-but-unusual factories cleared.

## Planted cheat and note

Open a prioritised held-out case, show its source rows and separate source families, then show the validated evidence-only note. No model decides the flag.

## Honest decoy

Show a legitimate operating-change factory with one signal. Explain why it stays outside the priority queue. This is not a legal clearance.

## Follow-up

Ask for the selected factory's dispatch gap by month. Display the guarded SELECT and returned table.

## Evaluation

Held-out factory-month recall is 84.42%, with 16.76% false positives. Annual factory recall is 99.31%, but annual false positives are 84.17%. There are 629 missed cheat months. This is not ready for operational taxpayer targeting. Seed A selected the threshold; seed B was held out. Open one recorded miss and its reason. Scale measurements are added only after they run.

## Limits and adaptation

Only synthetic data was used. An officer must review the evidence. Real bank and power records need authorised access, privacy controls, and calibration on validated cases. Germany is a proposed adaptation requiring local rules, not an implemented deployment.

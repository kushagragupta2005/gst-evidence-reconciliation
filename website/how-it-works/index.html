The method

Corroboration
before priority.

How separate pieces of evidence become a reason for an officer to review.

Independent records↓Conservation checks↓Source families↓SQL rules → priority queue↓Officer review

Production

Does the output
make sense?

Material-to-output and power proxies test declared production. Stock roll-forward checks opening stock, purchases, output, dispatch and closing stock. These checks share one production family.

Movement

Did the invoices
follow the goods?

Dispatch quantities are compared with invoice quantities. Reused e-way identifiers add a movement signal. These are synthetic document records, not independently observed truck journeys.

Supplier

Does the other side
tell the same story?

Purchase quantities are reconciled with supplier returns. Chain tracing records loops as context; a loop alone is not an independent family or proof of fraud.

Financial

Do the flows
fit the declarations?

Bank receipts and payments are compared with declared sales and purchases. Lumpy settlements can be legitimate, so financial discrepancies need context.

One rule.
Human judgment.

SQL decides priority.

At least two source families must exceed the frozen threshold. Several checks in one family still count as one source family.

The note explains.

An optional LLM can draft a note from evidence. The displayed notes use a deterministic fallback and a numeric validator. Neither decides who is flagged.

The officer judges.

A contradiction becomes a review lead. An officer checks the evidence, requests documents and decides what action is justified.

“Independent” describes the source grouping in the prototype. Causal independence has not been established.

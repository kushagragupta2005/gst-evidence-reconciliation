Data & ethics

Synthetic by design.
Explicit about access.

Where every record comes from, who can see it and why real use needs lawful access.

Generated here.
Not collected from taxpayers.

A seeded Python generator creates factories, suppliers, monthly stock and power records, invoices, purchase invoices, e-way bills, supplier returns, bank entries, machines and supporting documents. All records and verification statuses are synthetic.

Inspect the generator ↗

Planted cheats.

Hidden production, inflated ITC through supplier discrepancies, and e-way reuse are planted at different intensities. Evasive cases may remain visible in only one family.

Honest exceptions.

Machinery upgrades, product-mix changes, longer hours, seasonal stock build, new suppliers and lumpy bank flows test false alarms.

Labels kept apart.

Truth labels describe planted scenarios for offline scoring. They are not features in detector SQL and are not accessible through the restricted API.

Real access
would change the work.

The roadmap starts with tax-system data: invoices, e-way bills and supplier returns. Power and bank data would follow only under lawful, proportionate access.

Real use needs purpose limits, access auditing, retention controls, privacy assessment and human review before any action affecting a taxpayer.

No real taxpayer data has been evaluated. A synthetic “verified” document is generator metadata, not an independent verification service.
